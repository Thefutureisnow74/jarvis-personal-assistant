JARVIS

Putting your assistant on every screen — desktop and phone.
Before you start: Jarvis lives on your computer. Your Jarvis setup finished by showing you your personal address — it looks like http://YOUR-COMPUTER-IP:8787. You'll use it below. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the computer, and the computer must be awake — Jarvis sleeps when it does.

DESKTOP — THE FULL DISPLAY

  1. Your setup placed a JARVIS icon on your desktop and Start Menu — double-click it.
  2. It opens as its own window: your briefing, your objectives, the voice panel, and the living sphere of everything Jarvis knows.
  3. Talk to it three ways: say "Jarvis, …" out loud, press the HANDS-FREE button once and just speak, or type into the command box.

IPHONE

  1. Open Safari (it must be Safari — Apple's rule, not ours).
  2. Go to http://YOUR-COMPUTER-IP:8787/phone
  3. Tap the Share button — the square with the upward arrow, bottom center.
  4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  5. Tap Add. A JARVIS icon appears on your home screen.
Tapping it opens full-screen: the sphere of your assistant's knowledge, live, with the version it's running in the corner. Tap any name on the sphere to read that page.

ANDROID

  1. Open Chrome.
  2. Go to http://YOUR-COMPUTER-IP:8787/phone
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → "Add to Home Screen" → Add.

IF SOMETHING WON'T LOAD

"Can't connect" on the phone The computer is asleep, or the phone left the Wi-Fi network. Wake the computer, rejoin the network, try again.
It loads but looks stale Check the version number in the corner against your latest update note — pull down to refresh, or close and reopen the app.
Your computer's address changed Home routers occasionally hand out a new address. Re-run the address step from your setup guide, then re-add the home screen icon with the new one.
Loads on the computer but never on the phone Some routers isolate devices from each other ("AP isolation" or "guest network"). Turn that off for your main network, or contact support for the always-on option.
